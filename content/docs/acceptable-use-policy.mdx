# Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: January 25, 2025

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines acceptable use of Deonhub's platform, services, and resources.

## 2. Prohibited Activities

Users must NOT:

### 2.1. Content

- Post illegal or harmful content
- Share malicious software
- Upload inappropriate or offensive material
- Violate intellectual property rights
- Post misleading or fraudulent information

### 2.2. Behavior

- Harass or discriminate against others
- Impersonate other users
- Spam or send unsolicited messages
- Manipulate platform features
- Interfere with other users' activities

### 2.3. Technical

- Attempt to breach security measures
- Scan or test platform vulnerabilities
- Overload or flood the platform
- Use automated scripts without permission
- Reverse engineer platform features

## 3. Project Guidelines

### 3.1. Innovators Must:

- Provide clear project requirements
- Set realistic expectations
- Respect developer time and effort
- Maintain professional communication
- Honor payment agreements

### 3.2. Developers Must:

- Accurately represent skills
- Meet project deadlines
- Communicate progress regularly
- Deliver quality work
- Respect project confidentiality

## 4. Communication Standards

All users must:

- Be professional and respectful
- Use appropriate language
- Avoid harassment or discrimination
- Respect privacy and confidentiality
- Report inappropriate behavior

## 5. Content Standards

All content must be:

- Legal and appropriate
- Accurate and truthful
- Respectful of rights
- Professional in nature
- Relevant to the platform

## 6. Security Requirements

Users must:

- Use strong passwords
- Keep credentials confidential
- Report security concerns
- Log out from shared devices
- Use secure networks

## 7. Enforcement

### 7.1. Violations may result in:

- Warning notices
- Account suspension
- Account termination
- Legal action
- Reporting to authorities

### 7.2. Appeal Process

Users may appeal enforcement actions by contacting support.

## 8. Reporting Violations

Report violations to:
Email: support@deonhub.tz

## 9. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to update this policy as needed.

## 10. Questions

For questions about this policy, contact:
Email: support@deonhub.tz
