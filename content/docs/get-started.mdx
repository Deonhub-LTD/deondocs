## Quick Start Guide

### For Innovators

1. **Sign Up**

   - Visit deonhub.tz
   - Click **"Join as Innovator"**

2. **Create Your Project**

   - Click **"New Project"** and fill in the details accordingly.
   - Fill in the following fields:
     - **Project Name**: A clear and concise title for your project.
     - **Description**: Describe your innovative idea, including its purpose, target audience, key features, and potential impact. Be specific and paint a vivid picture of your project's vision.
     - **Budget**: Specify the estimated budget for your project.
     - **Deadline**: Set the expected completion date for the project.
   - Remember to tick **"Make this project public"** if you want other developers to view and apply for your project.
   - Click **"Continue"** to create your project.

3. **Connect**

   - Review developer applications
   - Choose a developer
   - Start collaboration

### For Developers

1. **Create Profile**

   - Join at deonhub.tz
   - Click "Join as Developer"
   - Add your skills and experience

2. **Find Projects**
   - Browse available projects
   - Apply to matching opportunities
   - Discuss with innovators

## Platform Features

- **Project Dashboard**: Manage your work
- **Collaboration**: Real-time communication
- **File Sharing**: Collaborate on documents
- **Task Tracking**: Monitor progress
