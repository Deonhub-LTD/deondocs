## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1. Personal Information

- Name and contact details
- Professional information
- Payment information
- Communication records
- Portfolio and work samples

### 1.2. Automatically Collected Information

- Device information
- IP address
- Browser type
- Usage patterns
- Cookies and similar technologies

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide and improve our services
- Process payments
- Match innovators with developers
- Send service updates
- Ensure platform security
- Analyze usage patterns
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. Information Sharing

We share information only:

- Between matched innovators and developers
- With service providers who assist our operations
- When required by law
- With your explicit consent

## 4. Data Security

We protect your data through:

- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Regular security audits
- Access controls
- Secure data storage
- Regular backups

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request data deletion
- Opt-out of marketing communications
- Export your data
- File a complaint

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your data:

- While your account is active
- As required by law
- For legitimate business purposes

## 7. Cookies

We use cookies to:

- Maintain your session
- Remember preferences
- Analyze usage patterns
- Improve user experience

## 8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from users under 18 years old.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed in:

- Tanzania
- Other countries with adequate data protection

## 10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We will notify users of significant changes to this policy.
