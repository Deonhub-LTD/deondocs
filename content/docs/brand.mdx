## Naming

"Deonhub" is the brand name and should always be written with a capital "D." It’s a single word and should never be spelled as "DeonHub," "deonhub," or any other variation.

## Logo

Use the Deonhub wordmark for consistent and stronger brand recognition. Ensure the logo remains unmodified—do not alter colors, proportions, or typography to maintain brand integrity.
