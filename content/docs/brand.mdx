## Naming

"Deonhub" is the brand name and should always be written with a capital "D." It's a single word and should never be spelled as "DeonHub," "deonhub," or any other variation.

## Logo

Use the Deonhub wordmark for consistent and stronger brand recognition. Ensure the logo remains unmodified—do not alter colors, proportions, or typography to maintain brand integrity.

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Minimum Size**: Maintain a minimum height of 32px for the wordmark and 24px for the icon to ensure legibility
2. **Clear Space**: Keep a padding area around the logo that's equal to the height of the "D" in the logo
3. **Background Contrast**: Always ensure sufficient contrast between the logo and its background
4. **File Formats**: Use PNG format for digital applications and vector formats (SVG) for print materials
5. **Color Usage**: Only use the provided black or white versions - do not alter the colors
